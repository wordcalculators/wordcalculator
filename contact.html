Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about contacting us

What information should I include in my support request? +
For faster assistance, please include: your name, email address, the specific tool you're using (if applicable), a clear description of the issue or question, any error messages you're receiving, and steps to reproduce the issue if it's a technical problem.
Do you offer phone support? +
Currently, we primarily offer support via email and through our contact form. This allows us to provide more detailed, documented assistance and serve more users efficiently. For complex issues, we can arrange a call if needed after initial email correspondence.
Can I request a new feature for WordCalculator? +
Absolutely! We welcome feature requests and suggestions. Please use the contact form and select "Feature Request" as the subject. Describe the feature you'd like to see, how it would help you, and any specific requirements you have. We regularly review user suggestions for future updates.
How long does it take to get a response? +
We strive to respond to all inquiries within 24 hours during business days. Technical support requests are typically answered within 12 hours, and urgent matters are prioritized for faster response. Responses may take slightly longer on weekends and holidays.
Do you provide support for enterprise customers? +
Yes, we offer dedicated support for enterprise customers and organizations. For enterprise support inquiries, please mention "Enterprise Support" in your message subject, and our dedicated team will provide priority assistance with custom solutions and dedicated contact points.
What if I need help with a specific tool? +
For tool-specific assistance, please mention the exact tool name in your message. You can also check our "How It Works" page for detailed guides on using each tool. Many common questions are answered in our documentation and tutorials available on the website.
