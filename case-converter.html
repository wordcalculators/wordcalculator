How this case converter works

This tool normalizes your text, cleans extra spaces, and then applies the case style you choose. It works completely in the browser, so you can safely use it for drafts, marketing copy, product descriptions, or client deliverables without sending anything to a server.

Core case styles

  • Sentence case – Only the first letter of each sentence is capitalized.
  • Title Case – Capitalizes important words, leaving small words like “of” or “and” lowercased in the middle of a title.
  • UPPERCASE / lowercase – Helpful for headings, buttons, and quick emphasis.
  • Capitalize Each Word – Every word starts with a capital letter, useful for short labels.
  • camelCase / PascalCase – Common in programming and hashtags, joining words without spaces.
  • snake_case / kebab-case – Use underscores or hyphens between words, popular in URLs and file names.

Cleaning text before you publish

The clean-up options can remove doubled spaces, normalize spacing after punctuation, and collapse multiple blank lines. This is especially helpful when you paste text from PDFs, documents, or emails that carry hidden formatting.

Case, readability, and SEO

Search engines don’t directly rank pages based on uppercase or lowercase, but casing strongly affects human readability. Clear, consistent casing helps users scan headings, meta descriptions, and on-page copy quickly, which indirectly supports better engagement metrics.

Using casing to support SEO

  • Headline clarity: Titles in Title Case or sentence case are easier to scan than ALL CAPS headlines.
  • Meta descriptions: Sentence case, with one or two key phrases highlighted, is usually the easiest to read in search results.
  • Button and UI text: Short labels in Title Case or Capitalize Each Word often feel more polished than mixed or random casing.
  • Code and URLs: camelCase, snake_case, and kebab-case improve consistency when naming files, slugs, and CSS classes.

Quick workflow tips

Draft your content in whatever style is most comfortable, then drop it into this converter at the end. You can generate multiple versions (for example, UPPERCASE for banner text and Title Case for blog headings) from the same source paragraph and keep your brand voice consistent across a whole site.
