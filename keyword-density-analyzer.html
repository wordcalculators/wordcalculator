How to Optimize Your Content

  • Keep primary keyword density between 1-2.5% for optimal results
  • Use secondary keywords naturally throughout the content
  • Avoid keyword stuffing - search engines penalize excessive repetition
  • Include keywords in headings, meta descriptions, and image alt text
  • Use synonyms and related terms to improve topical relevance
  • Regularly check density as you write and edit content
