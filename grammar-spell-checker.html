How this grammar & spell checker works

This tool runs a series of lightweight checks directly in your browser. It looks for common spelling mistakes, capitalization errors, missing sentence endings, long and hard-to-follow sentences, and small style issues such as double spaces or a lowercase “i” used as the pronoun “I”.

Because everything happens locally, your text never leaves the page or gets sent to an external API. That makes it a fast, privacy-friendly companion for quick edits, emails, blog posts, and on-page SEO tweaks.

What it checks

  • Spelling: flags common typos and misspellings.
  • Grammar: checks for missing capitalization at the start of sentences, missing punctuation at the end, and basic agreement problems such as a lowercase i where it should be I.
  • Style & clarity: highlights very long sentences, repeated spaces, and patterns that can make text harder to scan quickly.

SEO-style clarity score

The clarity score (0–100) is a simple, SEO-inspired indicator. It starts at 100 and subtracts small penalties for each spelling, grammar, and style issue. Text with short, clear sentences and few problems tends to score higher and is usually easier for both readers and search engines to understand.

Tips for using this tool

  • Paste a realistic section of your article, not just a single sentence.
  • Fix spelling and grammar issues first, then review style suggestions to improve clarity.
  • If the score is low because of long sentences, try splitting them into two or more shorter ones.

Limitations

This checker is intentionally lightweight. It does not fully replace AI-powered or professional editing tools, and it may miss nuanced grammar issues or context-specific word choices. For legal, medical, or very important business documents, always combine automated checks with a careful human review.
