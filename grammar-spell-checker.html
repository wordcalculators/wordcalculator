How this grammar & spell checker works

This tool runs a series of lightweight checks directly in your browser. It looks for common spelling mistakes, capitalization errors, missing sentence endings, long and hard-to-follow sentences, and small style issues such as double spaces or informal lowercase “i” used as a pronoun.

Because everything happens locally, your text never leaves the page or gets sent to an external API. That makes it a fast, privacy-friendly companion for quick edits, emails, blog posts, and on-page SEO tweaks.

What it checks

  • Spelling: flags common typos and misspellings.
  • Grammar: checks for missing capitalization at the start of sentences, missing punctuation at the end, and basic agreement problems such as lowercase i.
  • Style & clarity: highlights very long sentences, repeated spaces, and patterns that can make text harder to scan quickly.

SEO-style clarity score

The clarity score (0–100) starts at 100 and subtracts small penalties for each spelling, grammar, and style issue.

Tips for using this tool

  • Paste a realistic section of your article, not just a single sentence.
  • Fix spelling and grammar issues first, then review style suggestions.
  • Split very long sentences into two or more shorter ones.

Limitations

This checker is intentionally lightweight. It may miss nuanced grammar issues or context-specific word choices. For legal, medical, or very important business documents, always combine automated checks with a careful human review.
