Our Story

From a simple idea to a comprehensive suite of 30+ SEO tools

WordCalculator was founded in 2020 by Dipen Bishwas, an SEO expert who was frustrated with the complexity and high costs of existing SEO tools. Dipen believed that powerful SEO analysis should be accessible to everyone, not just large enterprises with big budgets.

What started as a simple word counter tool quickly evolved into a comprehensive platform for content optimization. Our users requested more features, and we listened. Today, WordCalculator offers over 30 specialized tools across 5 categories for keyword research, content analysis, competitor research, and SEO optimization.

We're proud to have helped thousands of digital marketers, bloggers, and website owners improve their online visibility. Our tools have been used to optimize content that ranks for millions of keywords across every major search engine.

Our journey continues as we develop new features and tools to help you succeed in the ever-evolving world of digital marketing and SEO.
