How to use Session History

Session History is a local session manager designed to help you keep track of results and inputs you used across tools. If you run multiple calculations, write drafts, test settings, or generate outputs, you can store a session record and return to it later without repeating the work.

What a "session" means

A session is a saved snapshot of what you did in a tool—such as tool name, timestamp, optional title, optional tags/notes, and an optional JSON payload (settings, results, and context). Tools can store different shapes of payloads depending on the tool.

Common use cases

  • Repeatable workflows: Save settings you use frequently, then reuse them later.
  • Auditing and comparisons: Compare outputs across sessions and verify changes over time.
  • Content and SEO work: Save drafts, counts, analysis snapshots, and before/after results.
  • Study and productivity: Keep a record of focus sessions, goals, and time tracking patterns.
  • Troubleshooting: Save inputs/results when reporting issues or validating fixes.

Privacy and storage

Sessions are stored locally on your device using browser storage. This is fast and private by default: sessions remain in your browser unless you manually export them or clear browser data. If you share your device, consider clearing history.

Exporting your sessions

  • JSON export: Best for backups and restoring complete session data (including payload).
  • CSV export: Best for spreadsheets and reporting—typically includes summary fields (title/tool/date/tags).

Best practices

  • Name sessions clearly: Titles and tags make sessions easy to find later.
  • Pin important entries: Keep your key sessions at the top.
  • Export periodically: If the sessions are valuable, export them for backup.
